# 1 Summary

The full processing chain ran on simulated data: calibration, on-board processing of {{evaluation.frames}}
sky frames, telemetry and ground decoding. The requirements that can be tested without hardware (PL-001,
PL-002, PL-003, PL-008) are met. One streak merged with a bright star; it was still detected with the
right direction, but its centre and length were biased. The next step is the real-sky trial, SKY-TP-001.

# 2 What was run

`payload/run_trial.sh`: the simulator (`payload/sim/`) produced 16 darks, 16 flats, 30 photon-transfer
pairs, 5 dark-ramp frames and {{evaluation.frames}} science frames of 512 x 512 pixels at 0.2 s, each with
about 60 stars; satellites crossed three of the frames. Sensor values used by the simulator are listed in
SKY-PDR-001 section 7.

# 3 Deviations from SKY-TP-001

| Step | Deviation | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| all | simulated sensor and sky instead of the real camera and sky | results show the software and method work; they say nothing about the real camera |
| 4 | no PPS hardware; time-stamps generated by the simulator | PL-004 not tested |
| 5 | no focusing step; the simulated PSF is fixed at 1.2 px sigma | none for the software test |
| 6 | catalogue comparison replaced by the simulator's truth table | S-2 and S-3 tested against truth, not a catalogue |
